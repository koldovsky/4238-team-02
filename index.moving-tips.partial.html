Moving Tips

We strive to make your moving process as comfortable as possible, so here is some advice from us.

The main tip is to relax and not to think of moving as a disaster that you have to overcome. People move houses and offices many times in their lives, so there is nothing complicated in moving, especially if you use professional services and plan such activities in advance.

Area Rugs

+

Vacuum your area rugs thoroughly before moving day. For large rugs, consider rolling them up and wrapping them in plastic to protect from moisture and dirt. Small rugs can be folded and packed in boxes. Label each rug with its intended room destination to make unpacking easier.

Furniture

+

Disassemble large furniture pieces when possible to save space and prevent damage. Keep all screws and hardware in labeled bags taped to the furniture. Wrap furniture in blankets or bubble wrap, and use furniture pads for extra protection during transport.

Houseplants

+

Water your plants a few days before moving, not the day of. This prevents soil from becoming too heavy or leaking. Pack plants in open boxes with newspaper for cushioning. For long-distance moves, consider giving plants to friends, as they may not survive the journey.

Heavy Items

+

Pack heavy items in small boxes to make them manageable to carry. Distribute weight evenly and never pack a box so heavy that it can't be safely lifted. Use proper lifting techniques: bend your knees, keep your back straight, and get help when needed.
